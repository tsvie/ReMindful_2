Privacy Policy for Re:Mindful

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Re:Mindful ("the App") handles your information. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our strict "Offline-First" and "Privacy by Design" approach.

1. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Re:Mindful operates completely offline. The App does not contain any tracking software, analytics tools, or third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not have access to any of your entries, goals, or moods.

2. Local Storage

All data you enter into the App (including your name, journal entries, goals, and customized settings) is stored exclusively and locally on your personal device using secure local storage mechanisms.

If you delete the App or clear your device's app data, your journal entries will be permanently deleted. You can manually delete all your data at any time using the "Delete all data" button within the App's settings.

3. Permissions

The App requests the following device permissions for specific functional purposes only:

  • Notifications: To provide you with local, daily reminders (if you actively enable them in the settings). These notifications are scheduled locally on your device and are not triggered by any external server.

4. PDF Export

If you use the PDF export feature, the App generates the document locally on your device and saves it directly to your device's Downloads folder using the native Android MediaStore API. No data is sent to a server to generate the document.

5. Medical Disclaimer

Re:Mindful is designed for personal growth and self-reflection. It is not a medical device, nor is it intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It does not replace professional therapy or a doctor.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the App operates offline, we encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon posting on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the GitHub repository.
